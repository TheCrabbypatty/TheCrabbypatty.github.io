Tomorrow Sun

A solar power forecast that says how much to trust it. A random forest model with a trust meter that is right 98.0% of the time on clear days and 68.7% on straddle days. The best battery rule built on it costs about $52 a year in wrong calls, against $164 for always trusting the sun, saving about $112 a year.

Built with Python, scikit-learn, and Google Colab.
