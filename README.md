## Projects

- **Tomorrow Sun** — a solar power forecast that says how much to trust it. A random forest model with a trust meter, right 98.0% of the time on clear days and 68.7% on straddle days. The best battery rule built on it costs about $52 a year in wrong calls, against $164 for always trusting the sun, saving about $112 a year. Excellence Award, Veritas AI Hackathon, November 2026, out of 1,000+ projects from 75+ countries, plus a $1,000 scholarship. Submitted to WAICY 2026.
- **AMC Simulator** — an AoPS-style practice simulator for the AMC 10, with a minute plan, a checkmark count tracker, and miss classification. Loads the 2024 AMC 10A and 10B. https://thecrabbypatty.github.io/amc-simulator/
- **Exit West Text Game** — Doors: A Project. An interactive text game based on Mohsin Hamid's *Exit West*. Eight choices, seven endings, built in plain JavaScript. Graded 9.5 out of 10 as a class summative. https://thecrabbypatty.github.io/exit-west-text/
- **NextOS** — a productivity dashboard with AI features and integrations with common productivity applications. Shown at NGN Hacks 2026.
- **VividGrasp** — an AI-based robotic arm simulator.
